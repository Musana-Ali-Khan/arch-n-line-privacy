Privacy Policy for Arch N Line

Effective date: August 3, 2026 · Last updated: August 3, 2026

Arch N Line ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is a cross-platform sketching and drafting app for architects and designers, developed by Musana Ali Khan. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you. It applies to the Arch N Line app on iOS and Android (package: com.quzafic.archnline).

By using Arch N Line, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and (optionally) an avatar image. You can sign up using email/password or Google Sign-In. We also store your subscription tier and account creation date.

1.2 Your Content

Arch N Line stores the projects you create — drawings, pages, layers, room scans, imported images, thumbnails, and custom symbols. This content is stored locally on your device first (offline-first design). If your plan includes cloud backup, this content is also synced to our secure cloud database, where access is restricted to your account only (and any collaborators you explicitly invite).

1.3 Camera, Photos, and Microphone

With your permission, the App accesses:

  • Camera — for augmented reality (AR) drawing/placement and room scanning, and to let you capture site photos.
  • Photo library — to import images as drawing layers or reference material, and to save/export your work.
  • Microphone — used as part of AR and room-capture sessions on supported devices.

Camera, motion-sensor, and microphone data used for AR and room scanning are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers, except as described in Section 1.5 below.

1.4 Location

With your permission, the App accesses your device's location (GPS) to center map-based site plans ("Map Projects") on your current location. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings; core drawing features will continue to work without it.

1.5 AI Floor Plan Scanning (Third-Party AI Processing)

If you use the "AI blueprint/floor-plan scan" feature, the floor-plan image you select is sent to Google's Gemini API (Generative Language API) for analysis, so the App can detect rooms and walls and convert them into editable drawing geometry. This is the only feature where your image content is sent to a third-party AI service. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy terms for the Generative Language API.

1.6 Purchases

Subscription purchases are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play through RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your payment card details. We only receive the resulting subscription/entitlement status (e.g., Free or Pro tier).

1.7 Device and Usage Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, and app version to maintain and improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, secure, and manage your account
  • To provide core app features — drawing, layers, symbol library, AR, room scanning, map projects, CAD/PDF export
  • To sync and back up your projects across your devices, if you have cloud backup enabled
  • To process AI floor-plan scans via Google's Gemini API, when you use that feature
  • To manage your subscription and entitlements via RevenueCat
  • To send you notification reminders you've scheduled within the App
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics/tracking SDKs. We share information only with the service providers necessary to operate the App:

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

Your content is stored locally on your device and, if you use cloud backup, on Supabase's cloud infrastructure with row-level security so only you (and any collaborators you invite) can access it. We retain account and project data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your synced cloud data at any time via Settings → Delete cloud backup & projects. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your account information and cloud-stored project data from our servers within 30 days. Content stored locally on your device remains on that device under your control until you delete the App or clear its data.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Disable camera, photo, microphone, or location access at any time in device settings
  • Delete your cloud-synced projects and backup via in-app Settings
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by contacting us below
  • Delete your account entirely by contacting us

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information — for example, the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your data, and the right to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9 and we will respond in accordance with applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

Arch N Line is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
