To delete your Arch N Line account and all associated data, send us an email request. We review and process every request manually to make sure deletions are safe and intentional.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us at musanaali02@gmail.com from the address associated with your Arch N Line account (or include that email in your message).
  2. Use the subject line "Delete My Account."

What gets deleted

  • Your account details (email, display name, avatar)
  • All projects, pages, layers, drawings, room scans, and custom symbols in your cloud backup

We process deletion requests within 30 days. Content stored locally on your device is under your control at all times and is removed when you delete the app or clear its data — you don't need to wait for us for that part.
